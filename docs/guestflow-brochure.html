What is GuestFlow Pro?

A complete digital system for the modern hotel

Built specifically for independent hotels and small hotel chains who want to modernise the guest experience without managing multiple tools.

GuestFlow Pro replaces paper check-in forms, printed city guides, manual review collection, and disconnected marketing lists with a single branded platform that works on every guest's phone.

Hotel managers access everything from one dashboard — guest registrations, booking requests, service orders, review scores, and marketing analytics — without switching between different tools or providers.

The entire platform is white-labelled to your hotel: your logo, your brand color, your welcome message. Guests never see "GuestFlow" — they see your hotel.

The old way
  • Paper registration cards stored in folders
  • Staff manually chasing reviews after checkout
  • Guests asking reception for restaurant tips
  • Marketing lists built by hand from email signups
  • No visibility into upsell revenue or guest trends
With GuestFlow Pro
  • Guest checks in on phone before arriving
  • Review requests sent automatically post-checkout
  • AI-powered city guide on guest's phone at all times
  • Marketing opt-ins captured at registration
  • Full dashboard with live stats and export tools

Complete Guest Journey
End-to-End Guest Flow

Every step of the guest experience, connected

From the first enquiry to a repeat booking — GuestFlow Pro manages the full journey. 8 of 9 steps are live; checkout and repeat booking are in development.

🏨

Step 1 — Booking Request

Guest submits a room enquiry via the hotel's public page. Hotel receives it in the Booking Requests dashboard and confirms or declines with one click. Notes added internally, never shown to the guest.

Live
📨

Step 2 — Pre-arrival Check-in Link

Hotel sends a unique digital check-in link via WhatsApp or email. Guest receives a branded registration form on their phone — no app download required.

Live
✅

Step 3 — Online Registration

Guest completes full registration before arrival: name, nationality, passport number, photo, digital signature, GDPR consent, and optional marketing opt-in. All stored securely in the hotel dashboard.

Live
🗺️

Step 4 — Digital Concierge

Guest accesses the hotel's branded concierge page: AI-described restaurants, parking, nightlife, tours, and local attractions — powered by Google Places and GetYourGuide.

Live
🛎️

Step 5 — In-stay Upsells

From the same concierge page, guests order hotel services: room service, spa treatments, airport transfers, late checkout. Payments processed at point of order. Orders visible in hotel dashboard.

Live
⭐

Step 6 — Review Collection

Hotel sends branded review link post-checkout. Guest rates stay privately (1–5 stars). Satisfied guests redirected to Google or TripAdvisor to post publicly — growing hotel's reputation automatically.

Live
🏁

Step 7 — Checkout

Automated checkout reminder sent to guest. Hotel staff notified of departures. Checkout tracked in dashboard alongside full stay summary.

Coming Soon
📣

Step 8 — Future Promotions

Opted-in guests stored in marketing list. Export as CSV or pull via API. Analytics show opt-in rate, nationality breakdown, and 6-month trends. Ready for email campaign tools.

Live
🔁

Step 9 — Repeat Booking

Personalised re-engagement message sent to past guests with a return offer. The loop closes — a one-time guest becomes a loyal repeat customer.

Coming Soon
